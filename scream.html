The Scream

Artist: Edvard Munch

Year: 1893

The Scream is a famous expressionist painting by Edvard Munch. It shows a figure standing on a bridge with a dramatic, colourful sky in the background. The painting represents feelings of anxiety, fear, and emotional distress.
