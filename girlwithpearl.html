Girl with a Pearl Earring

Artist: Johannes Vermeer

Year: Around 1665

Girl with a Pearl Earring is a famous painting by Johannes Vermeer. It shows a young girl wearing a blue and yellow headscarf and a large pearl earring. The painting is known for its beautiful lighting and realistic appearance.
